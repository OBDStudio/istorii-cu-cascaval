1. What are cookies

Cookies are small files stored on a user's device when they visit a website.

They may be used to ensure the website functions correctly, remember preferences, analyse website traffic and, in certain cases, support marketing and advertising activities.

2. Types of cookies we may use

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the operation and security of the website and cannot normally be disabled through the consent system.

They may be used for:

  • technical operation of the website;
  • security;
  • session management;
  • remembering cookie preferences;
  • protecting forms against spam or abuse.

Functional cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember certain user preferences and provide improved functionality.

Analytics and statistics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the website, including:

  • which pages are visited;
  • how users arrive on the website;
  • how long they spend on certain pages;
  • how they interact with website elements.

These cookies will be used according to the user's preferences and consent, where required.

Marketing and advertising cookies

The website may use marketing technologies to measure campaign effectiveness, create audiences and display more relevant advertising.

These technologies may be provided by external advertising or social media platforms.

Marketing cookies will only be activated in accordance with user preferences and applicable legal requirements.

3. WordPress and plugins

The website is built using WordPress and may use plugins and external services for:

  • forms;
  • security;
  • analytics;
  • performance optimisation;
  • social media integrations;
  • marketing and advertising;
  • cookie consent management.

Some plugins may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

The cookies used by the website may therefore change when plugins or external services are installed, removed or updated.

4. Third-party cookies

Certain website features may be provided by external services such as analytics, advertising, social media, form or security providers.

These providers may place or access their own cookies in accordance with their respective privacy and cookie policies.

Where a third-party service is not strictly necessary, it will be activated according to the user's consent where required by applicable law.

5. Managing consent

When visiting the website for the first time, users may be shown a cookie consent banner.

Users may be able to:

  • accept all cookies;
  • reject non-essential cookies;
  • select specific categories;
  • change their preferences later.

Rejecting optional cookies will not prevent access to the essential functions of the website, although certain additional features may be limited.

6. Cookie duration

Cookies may be:

Session cookies – generally deleted when the browser is closed.

Persistent cookies – remain on the device for a specified period or until manually deleted.

The exact duration depends on the type of cookie and the service using it.

7. Browser settings

Users may also delete or block cookies through their browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some website features.

8. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when:

  • WordPress plugins are installed or removed;
  • new analytics or marketing services are introduced;
  • the way cookies are used changes;
  • relevant legal or technical requirements change.

The updated version will be published on this page.
